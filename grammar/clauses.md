# Clauses

This guide introduces a simple English clause for a beginner learner.

## Attributive Clause (Relative Clause)

An attributive clause adds information about a person, animal, or thing. It comes directly after the word it describes.

### Use **who** for people

- He is the boy who eats breakfast every day.
- I have a friend who likes football.
- She is the teacher who helps me.

### Use **that** for animals or things

- This is the dog that runs very fast.
- I like the book that has a blue cover.
- Here is the toy that makes music.

We can put the two parts together:

- I know the girl. She can sing.
- I know the girl **who can sing**.

## Quick Practice

Complete the sentence.

1. This is the dog ___ runs fast. (`who` / `that`)

**Answer:** 1. that.
